Frequently asked questions

What services does Dynamo Building Solutions offer?

Commercial building and plaza maintenance, overhead and garage doors, fire-rated doors, locks and hardware, broken glass and window repair, barrier gates and access systems, on-site welding and metal repair, loading dock repairs, and 24/7 emergency service requests.

Where do you work?

Halton Hills, Milton, Mississauga, Brampton, Toronto and the wider GTA and surrounding areas.

How much does a service visit cost?

A service call is $89.00 per visit and labour is $90.00 per technician per hour. The minimum initial charge is $449.00 before applicable taxes. Parts, materials and additional work are quoted separately.

Do you take emergency calls?

We take 24/7 emergency service requests on 647-570-1324. Emergency and after-hours work may require a separate estimate.
