Frequently asked questions

How is the minimum initial charge worked out?

It is one $89.00 service call plus two technicians for two hours at $90.00 per technician per hour: $89.00 + (2 x 2 x $90.00) = $449.00, before applicable taxes.

What is not included in these rates?

Parts, materials and any additional work are quoted separately, and applicable taxes are added.

Are emergency and after-hours calls priced the same way?

Emergency and after-hours work may require a separate estimate. Ask when you call.

How can I get an estimate before a visit?

Send the property address, a description of the issue and photos. That is the fastest way to a useful estimate.

Need a repair or a service call?

Send the address, a description of the issue and photos for a faster estimate, or call and speak to the team.
