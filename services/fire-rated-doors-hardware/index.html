Fire-rated doors, locks and commercial door hardware

Entry and exit doors take more daily abuse than almost anything else in a building. Dynamo repairs commercial metal doors, frames and hardware, including fire-rated doors, for buildings, plazas and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

That covers the door itself and the parts that make it work: closers, hinges, locks, handles, panic hardware and electric strikes.

Typical call-outs

  • A door that does not close fully or will not latch
  • A damaged hinge, frame or door edge
  • A panic bar that sticks or does not release
  • A lock or handle that has failed
  • An electric strike that does not release or does not hold
A fire-rated door, its frame and its hardware work together as a labelled assembly. Repairs should use compatible parts and should not alter the door in ways that affect its rating. If you are unsure whether a door is rated, check its label and the building's requirements before work starts. Questions about what a building's fire code requires belong with the building's fire-protection consultant or the local authority.

Frequently asked questions

Can you replace a closer or hinge on a fire-rated door?

Yes. Closers, hinges, locks, handles and panic hardware are part of what we repair. Replacement parts need to suit the rated assembly, and a fire-rated assembly must keep its required rating after the repair.

What is an electric strike?

It is an electrically controlled part of the door frame that releases the latch so a door can be opened from a card reader, keypad or remote release. When one fails, the door may stay locked or fail to hold. We repair and replace electric strikes.

My door will not latch. What should I send you?

Photos of the door edge, latch and frame, and a short description of when it started. A door that does not latch is often a closer adjustment, a worn latch or a hinge or frame that has moved.
