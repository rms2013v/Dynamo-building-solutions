Commercial overhead and garage door repair

A commercial overhead door that will not open or close stops deliveries, locks staff out or leaves a building unsecured. Dynamo repairs, services and installs commercial overhead and garage doors for buildings, plazas, warehouses and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

We work on the door, its hardware and its automatic opener, so one visit can cover the whole problem rather than part of it.

What we repair and install

  • Springs, tracks, rollers, cables and hardware
  • Automatic openers and operators
  • Door installation and replacement
  • Damage from vehicle or equipment contact
  • Preventive maintenance and adjustment

Typical call-outs

  • The door will not open or close
  • The door has come off its track
  • A broken spring: the door is suddenly heavy or will not lift
  • The opener hums or clicks but the door does not move
  • A remote or wall control that no longer responds
Door springs and cables hold a lot of tension and should be left to a technician. If a door is stuck open, keep people and vehicles clear of it until it is secured.

Frequently asked questions

What causes a commercial overhead door to stop working?

Common causes are a broken spring, a door off its track, worn rollers or cables, and an opener fault. A technician needs to see the door to say which, and photos of the door plus any noise or error you noticed help us prepare.

Can you repair the opener as well as the door?

Yes. We repair and replace automatic openers and operators as well as the door hardware.

My door is stuck open. What should I do?

Keep people and vehicles clear and call 647-570-1324 with the address and a photo. Emergency and after-hours work may require a separate estimate, so ask when you call.
