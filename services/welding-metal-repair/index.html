On-site welding and metal repair for commercial properties

Many building repairs end in a broken piece of metal: a sheared hinge, a bent frame, a cracked bracket, a gate that has dropped. Dynamo offers on-site welding and steel repair for commercial buildings, plazas and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

Having welding in the same team as the door, glass and gate work means the damage and the repair can be assessed together instead of passed between trades.

What we weld and reinforce

  • Hinges and brackets
  • Gates and gate frames
  • Door frames and metal reinforcement
  • Steel repairs and custom site solutions

Typical call-outs

  • A cracked or sheared hinge
  • A bent or damaged door frame
  • A gate or railing that has failed at a weld or bracket
  • A custom bracket or reinforcement for a particular site

Frequently asked questions

Can you weld on site?

Yes. On-site welding and steel repair is one of our services, so in many cases the part does not need to be removed and taken to a shop.

Can you reinforce a frame or gate that keeps failing?

Yes. Metal reinforcement and custom site solutions are part of the service. Photos of the failure point and a note on how often it happens help us quote.
