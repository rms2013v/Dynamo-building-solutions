Loading dock repairs and maintenance

A loading dock that is out of service holds up every delivery behind it. Dynamo repairs and maintains commercial loading dock doors, dock-area hardware and equipment for warehouses, plazas and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

Dock areas take heavy impact from trucks and equipment, so repairs often involve a door and the metal around it. We handle the door work and the welding together.

What we handle

  • Loading dock doors and their hardware
  • Dock-area service doors and access points
  • Dock equipment repairs and maintenance
  • Metal frame and bracket repairs after impact

Typical call-outs

  • A dock door damaged by a truck or equipment
  • A door that will not operate
  • Loose or damaged hardware around the dock
  • Bent metal framing at the opening

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair dock doors that have been hit by a vehicle?

Yes. Send photos of the door, the tracks and the frame. Impact damage often involves the metal around the door, which we can weld and reinforce on site.

Do you offer maintenance for dock areas, not only repairs?

Yes. Maintenance is part of the service for loading dock doors, hardware and related equipment.

Do you cover dock service doors and access points?

Yes. Service doors and other access points in and around the dock area are part of the work.
