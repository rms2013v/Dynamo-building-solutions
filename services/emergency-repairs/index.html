24/7 emergency building maintenance and repairs

Some repairs cannot wait for the next business day: a door that cannot be locked, glass that has left a building open, a gate or barrier that is blocking access. Dynamo takes 24/7 emergency service requests for commercial buildings, plazas and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

Emergency and after-hours work may require a separate estimate, so ask about it when you call.

Call 647-570-1324Email a request

Emergency work we take

  • Damaged commercial doors
  • Broken glass and open storefronts
  • Faulty locks and hardware
  • Gates and barriers that are stuck or damaged
  • Other urgent property repairs

When you call, have ready

  • The property address
  • What happened and whether the building can be secured
  • A photo of the damage, if it is safe to take one
  • A contact on site and the best number to reach them
If there is a fire, an injury or immediate danger, call 911 first. Then call Dynamo for the building repair.

How to get service

Call 647-570-1324 or email dynamoquotesolution@gmail.com with the property address, a description of the issue, photos before work and your preferred time for the visit. A service call is $89.00 per visit and labour is $90.00 per technician per hour; parts, materials and additional work are quoted separately. See the full rates.

Frequently asked questions

Are you available 24/7?

We take 24/7 emergency service requests. Call 647-570-1324. Emergency and after-hours work may require a separate estimate, so ask when you call.

What counts as an emergency?

Typical examples are a door that cannot be locked, broken glass that leaves a building open, a stuck or damaged gate or barrier blocking access, and urgent repairs that put the property at risk.

What should I send?

The property address, a short description and photos if it is safe to take them. You can call, or email the details for the fastest estimate.
