Barrier gate and access system repair

When a parking barrier or gate stops working, tenants cannot get in, or a site cannot be secured. Dynamo repairs parking barrier arms, gate operators, entry systems and other commercial access equipment for buildings, plazas and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

Because we also weld, damaged arms, posts, brackets and gate frames can often be looked at together with the operator and the access equipment.

What we repair

  • Parking barrier arms
  • Gate operators
  • Entry systems and commercial access-related equipment
  • Gate frames, brackets and hinges (with on-site welding)

Typical call-outs

  • A barrier arm struck or broken by a vehicle
  • A gate that will not open or close
  • An operator that is noisy, slow or stuck
  • An entry system that no longer responds

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair a barrier arm that has been hit?

Barrier arm and operator repairs are part of our service. Send photos of the arm, the post or housing and the surrounding area so we can plan the visit.

Do you repair damaged gate frames?

Yes. We weld on site, so bent frames, failed brackets and hinges can be repaired or reinforced during the same visit where possible.

What should I tell you about the access system?

The make and model if you know it, what it controls and what it is doing, for example no response, intermittent response or a fault light. Photos of the unit help.
