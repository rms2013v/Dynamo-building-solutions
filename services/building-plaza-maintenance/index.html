Commercial building and plaza maintenance

Dynamo Building Solutions looks after commercial buildings, shopping plazas, warehouses and managed properties in Halton Hills, Toronto and the wider GTA. Instead of calling a separate trade for every door, lock, pane of glass and gate, owners and property managers have one contact for general maintenance and repairs.

Our team's background is in doors, hardware, automatic operators, glass, access systems and welding, which often means fewer separate contractors to coordinate when a property has several things wrong at once.

What we look after

  • General maintenance and repairs for commercial buildings
  • Shopping plaza entrances, service doors and common areas
  • Warehouses, industrial units and managed properties
  • Door, glass, hardware, gate and metal repairs under one contact
  • Ongoing support and preventive maintenance visits

Typical call-outs

  • A tenant reports a door that will not close or latch
  • An entrance or service door damaged by an impact
  • A gate or barrier arm that is out of service
  • Broken glass that needs replacing or securing
  • Metal frames, brackets or railings that need repair
