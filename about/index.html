Who we are

DYNAMO BUILDING SOLUTIONS INC. is a young, ambitious company built on the experience of skilled tradespeople. Our team brings 15–20 years of hands-on industry experience in commercial building maintenance, door and hardware repairs, automatic operators, welding, glass, access systems and emergency service.

We combine established craftsmanship with a fresh, customer-focused approach, delivering practical solutions, clear communication and professional workmanship for commercial buildings, plazas and managed properties across Halton Hills, Toronto and the GTA.

Why work with us?

  • Skilled professionals with 15-20 years of hands-on experience
  • Commercial-property-focused service for buildings, plazas and facility managers
  • One contact for multiple service categories
  • Clear communication and responsive service
  • Wider service range including welding and metal repair
